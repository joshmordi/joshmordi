# Ifeanyi Mordi

UX researcher and PhD student in Rhetoric and Professional Communication at New Mexico State University.

I study how people use complex systems, from university portals to AI interfaces. My focus is ethical usability: making digital systems fair, transparent, and accountable to the people who use them.

## Focus areas

- UX research and usability testing
- Heuristic evaluation and accessibility reviews
- Ethical AI and AI governance
- Technical communication and content strategy

## Methods and tools

- Methods: usability testing, heuristic evaluation, interviews, surveys, task analysis
- Code: Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS
- Analysis: SUS scoring, task success rates, qualitative coding

## Selected work

- **Journal article:** "Ethical Usability: Technical Communication Strategies to Addressing User-centered Challenges in E-governance," *Technical Communication and Social Justice*, 4(1), 2026.
- **Poster, second place:** "A Critical Socio-Technical Usability Analysis of the NMSU Portal," Graduate Research and Arts Symposium, NMSU, 2025.
