# Hi, I'm Ifeanyi Mordi

UX researcher and PhD student in Rhetoric and Professional Communication at New Mexico State University. I study how people use complex systems, from university portals to AI interfaces. My focus is ethical usability: making digital systems fair, transparent, and accountable to the people who use them.

## What I do

- Usability testing and UX research
- Heuristic evaluation and accessibility reviews
- Ethical AI and AI governance
- Technical communication and content strategy
